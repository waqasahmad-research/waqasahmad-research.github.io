Research Portfolio

Research

My research integrates sustainable energy, thermochemical conversion, waste valorization, biochar, hydrogen technologies, advanced materials and environmental characterization. The work combines experimental engineering with materials and environmental analysis to develop practical pathways for resource recovery and low-carbon energy systems.

Research Projects

Selected Research Projects

Selected research programs spanning industry-funded biochar development, thermochemical biomass conversion, municipal waste-to-energy and anaerobic digestion.

01
INDUSTRY-FUNDED RESEARCH

Acidic Biochar Production for Soil Amendment & Waste Valorization

Industry-linked research focused on the production and characterization of engineered acidic biochar through pyrolysis, with emphasis on waste valorization and potential soil amendment applications.

The work involved pyrolysis reactor operation, production and characterization of biochar, bio-oil and gaseous products, assessment of heavy metals and organic contaminants, and physicochemical characterization of engineered biochar.

Industry Partner: Emirates Global Aluminium (EGA) | Approx. Project Value: AED 1.6 million
02
THERMOCHEMICAL CONVERSION

Salicornia & Date Palm Co-Pyrolysis

Experimental investigation of the co-pyrolysis of Salicornia bigelovii and date palm biomass for integrated bioenergy production and biochar development in arid and semi-arid regions.

The research examines the influence of process temperature and feedstock interaction on biochar, bio-oil and gaseous products, together with energy recovery, carbon stability and environmental safety of the resulting biochar.

Selected publication

W. Ahmad et al., “Co-Pyrolysis of Date Palm Waste and Salicornia Bigelovii: Insights for Bioenergy Development in Arid and Semi-Arid Regions,” ACS Omega, 2024.

03
USAID-FUNDED RESEARCH

Anaerobic Digestion, Biogas Production & Methane Refinement

Research involving the design, fabrication and operation of semi-continuous stirred-tank reactors for anaerobic digestion of poultry manure and renewable biogas production.

Experimental activities included reactor operation, controlled feeding, biogas production and gas chromatographic analysis, followed by investigation of methane refinement using gas purification and scrubbing approaches.

Research Environment: NUST | Funding Context: USAID
Related publication

“Methane refinement by iron oxide, packed-column water scrubbing, and activated charcoal scrubbing techniques: Process optimization.”

04
WASTE-TO-ENERGY

Municipal Solid Waste-to-Energy & Solid Recovered Fuels

Research focused on converting municipal and agricultural waste streams into useful energy resources through solid recovered fuels, refuse-derived fuels and biomass densification.

The work encompasses waste characterization, proximate and elemental analysis, fuel-quality assessment, energy recovery, economic feasibility and policy implications of sustainable waste-to-energy systems.

Research outputs include

Solid recovered fuel characterization, municipal solid waste-to-energy assessment, biomass pelletization and sustainable waste-management studies.

Research Directions

Current Research Directions

These interconnected research directions extend my work in thermochemical conversion and materials engineering toward emerging low-carbon technologies and environmentally responsible engineering.

Hydrogen production, storage and utilization; electrocatalytic hydrogen evolution; biohydrogen; electrolyzer and fuel-cell technologies; and integrated low-carbon energy systems.

High- and medium-entropy alloys, functional materials, electrocatalytic materials, surface modification and corrosion-resistant engineering systems.
