About

Engineering research with a sustainability focus.

I am an Assistant Professor of Mechanical Engineering at Amity University Dubai with a PhD in Materials Science and Engineering from the American University of Sharjah.

My research focuses on sustainable energy systems, thermochemical conversion of solid wastes, waste-to-energy, biochar-based resource recovery and low-carbon technologies. My work combines experimental research, materials characterization and environmental assessment to investigate practical pathways for resource recovery and sustainable energy production.

Alongside research, I teach and mentor engineering students across areas including thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, engineering materials, basic mechanics, engineering graphics and workshop practices.

Research

Research Areas

My research combines mechanical engineering, materials science and environmental engineering to address challenges in sustainable energy conversion and resource recovery.

01

Thermochemical Conversion

Pyrolysis and thermal processing of biomass and solid wastes for recovery of biochar, bio-oil and gaseous energy products.

02

Biochar & Resource Recovery

Engineering and characterization of biochar for sustainable waste valorization, carbon management and soil-related applications.

03

Waste-to-Energy

Investigation of energy recovery pathways from municipal, agricultural and other waste streams.

04

Hydrogen & Clean Energy

Research interests in hydrogen production, electrocatalysis, biohydrogen and emerging low-carbon energy systems.
