Engineering Education

Teaching & Mentoring

My teaching focuses on building strong engineering fundamentals while connecting theory with physical understanding, experimentation and real-world engineering applications. I aim to develop students who can move confidently from fundamental principles to analysis, design, laboratory investigation and engineering problem-solving.

Teaching Philosophy

From engineering fundamentals to practical problem-solving.

I view engineering education as a combination of conceptual understanding, analytical reasoning and practical application. Students should understand not only how to apply an equation, but also the physical principles behind it and the engineering decisions that follow from the result.

My teaching therefore integrates theory, numerical problem-solving, laboratory experimentation, engineering examples and project-based activities. Wherever possible, I connect classroom concepts with energy systems, mechanical processes, materials and real engineering applications.

Assessment and feedback are used not simply to measure performance, but to identify areas where students need further development and to support continuous improvement in learning.
